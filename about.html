Our Journey

Built on Stone,
Driven by Passion

StoneCraft began in 2005 as a small family workshop with a single bridge saw and an unwavering commitment to quality. What started as a two-person operation has grown into a full-service fabrication facility — but our core values remain unchanged.

We believe that stone is more than a building material. It is a piece of the earth, formed over millions of years, waiting to become part of your story. Every slab we touch is treated with the respect it deserves — measured twice, cut once, and finished by hand.

Today, our team of 24 skilled craftsmen and women serve homeowners, designers, builders, and developers across the region. From single bathroom vanities to complete hotel kitchens, we bring the same precision and care to every project.

"

We don't just cut stone. We reveal the beauty that was always there.

— Marcus Chen, Founder
Milestones

Two Decades of Craft

Genesis 2005

The Workshop Opens

Founded in a modest 1,200 sq ft workshop with a single manual bridge saw, immense grit, and an uncompromising standard for natural stonework.

2010 Innovation

CNC Precision Arrives

Pioneered 5-axis robotic bridge saw & waterjet cutting, delivering millimeter-exact mitered edges and seamless vein-matching technology.

Expansion 2015

Flagship Showroom Opens

Unveiled an 8,000 sq ft indoor slab gallery with over 300 live full-size slabs under curated 5000K museum-grade lighting.

2020 Sustainability

Eco-Water Recycling Initiative

Installed a closed-loop water treatment system recycling 98% of industrial shop water, and introduced certified carbon-neutral quartz surfaces.

Present Day 2025

Two Decades Strong

Proudly celebrating 20 years, 1,200+ residential & commercial projects completed, and remaining family-owned with an enduring love for artisan stone.
